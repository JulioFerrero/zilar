# Zilar brand marks

- `icon.svg`, `icon-1024.png`: app icon, a silver folded-metal Z on a dark rounded square (transparent corners).
- `logo-black.svg`, `logo-white.svg`: flat Z mark, four facets, for light and dark backgrounds.
- `logo-bubble-black.svg`, `logo-bubble-white.svg`: the Z cut out of a chat bubble, a flat mark for small sizes.

Colours: dark `#1b1e24` to `#434a56`, silver `#7f8994` to `#ffffff`. Zilar is Basque for silver.
