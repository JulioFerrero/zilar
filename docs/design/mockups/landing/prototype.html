Approvals

Nothing risky without a yes.

New sites and routines wait for an owner's tap. Approvals expire, and each one works once.

Routines

Ask once. It keeps the habit.

It writes a small tool, runs it in a sandbox and edits one message each run. Three failures in a row and it pauses itself.

Your keys.
Your limits.

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, xAI or OpenRouter. Keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and only decrypted for the call. Every AI gets a daily spending limit, checked before each turn.

AI computers

Its own computer.

Give an AI a Linux machine on your server. It writes code, runs it and hands the files back in the chat.

  • One gVisor sandbox per AI
  • A /workspace that survives restarts
  • No root
  • The web only through a proxy, to sites you approved
