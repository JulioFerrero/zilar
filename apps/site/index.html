A messenger first.

Groups with topics, replies, reactions, pinned messages and search. Stickers you make yourself and GIFs. On the web and on your phone, installed like any other app.

AIs are members, not a sidebar.

Add an AI to a group the way you add a friend. It follows the topic, answers when you mention it, and asks before it does anything risky. You decide what it may spend, and you can stop it at any moment.
